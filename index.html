Swedish West Coast Trophic Levels -- Habitat Suitability Results

Winter gains, summer losses. Averaged over the whole year, habitat suitability rises slightly for most trophic roles until 2099. Split by season, the picture is different: in winter (December-March) every role gains, because warmer winters lift conditions above the species' cold limits; in summer (June-September) almost every species loses (under SSP5-8.5, 66 of 71 species by 2080-2099). The whole-year increase is therefore winter gains outweighing summer losses, not an overall improvement.

How the projections are made (delta method). The climate-model ensemble (FutureMares, CMIP6, ~9 km) is only used for how much temperature, salinity and oxygen change from today to each future year. That change is added to the real, high-resolution Copernicus ocean data (~2 km, 39 depth levels), re-using real years so that natural year-to-year variability is kept. Scenarios: SSP2-4.5 (intermediate) and SSP5-8.5 (high emissions).
